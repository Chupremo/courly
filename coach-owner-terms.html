Coach Terms & Conditions

Version 2026-11-09-coach-v2 · Please review before publishing a coach profile.

1. Free coach listing

Courtly provides coach profiles and coaching-session management without monthly subscription, setup or upfront listing fees.

2. Booker Convenience Fee

A ₱25 Courtly Convenience Fee is charged to the booker for each successful coaching booking. It is added on top of the published coaching session price and is not deducted from the coach's coaching rate.

3. Direct player payments

Players pay the coaching session price plus the ₱25 Courtly Convenience Fee through the coach's configured payment method. Coaches must verify the full received amount and booking reference before confirming the session.

4. Convenience Fee remittance

Where the coach receives the booker's payment directly, the ₱25 Courtly Convenience Fee collected with the booking is recorded separately and may be included in Courtly's weekly remittance statement. It is collected from the booker and is not a charge against the coach's coaching earnings.

5. Overdue fee and listing suspension

If an undisputed invoice remains unpaid after the 72-hour deadline, Courtly may temporarily unpublish the coach profile and disable new coaching bookings. The coach retains access to their portal, records and existing confirmed sessions. Existing sessions should be honored unless resolved under the applicable cancellation policy.

6. Verified coach badge

Only Courtly administrators may grant or remove the Verified Coach badge. Coaches must provide accurate details and any verification evidence requested by Courtly.

7. Coach responsibilities

Coaches are responsible for accurate profile information, availability, pricing, payment details, professional conduct, player safety, honoring confirmed sessions, cancellations and timely remittance of any booker-paid Convenience Fees collected on Courtly's behalf.

8. Refunds and disputes

Coaches must handle player refunds and disputes promptly and accurately. Courtly may apply fee credits for verified refunded sessions according to the platform's published fee rules.

9. Acceptance

By creating and publishing a coach account, the coach confirms that they have read and accepted these terms.
