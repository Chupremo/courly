Before you pay

Check the date, time, venue or coach, payment recipient and total. A checkout timer means your selection is held temporarily; it is not a confirmed booking.

I paid, but my booking is not confirmed.

Check your dashboard first. If the status says “Awaiting verification”, the venue or coach still needs to review your proof. If the hold expired, do not pay again. Contact the recipient with your booking reference, payment reference, amount and time paid. Payment transfers cannot be reversed by changing a booking status.

I need to cancel, reschedule or ask for a refund.

Contact the venue or coach using the details on their Courtly listing. Ask them to confirm the applicable cancellation rules, available alternative time, and any refund amount and method. Courtly does not automatically transfer money back when a booking is cancelled or a receipt is rejected.

What is the Convenience Fee?

Court rentals have an owner-paid ₱10 fee per booked court-hour, which is not added to the player's court rental total. Paid Open Play has a ₱10 player-paid fee shown at checkout. Free Open Play has no Convenience Fee. Coaching has a ₱50 coach-paid fee per booking, which is not added to the player's session price.

I didn't receive an email.

Check your spam folder and that you used the right account email. Your dashboard remains the place to check booking status even when an email is delayed. For password recovery, request a new link and use the most recent email.
