Venue Owner Terms & Conditions

Effective version: 2026-11-05-v1

These terms explain how Courtly's free venue listing, booking tools, transaction-based platform fees, weekly invoicing and payment suspension process work. By accepting these terms in the Owner Portal, the venue owner agrees to the obligations below.

1. Free venue listing

Courtly provides eligible venue owners with access to venue listings and booking-management tools without monthly subscription fees, setup fees, registration fees or upfront platform charges. Courtly earns revenue from the transaction-based fees described below.

2. Court rental platform fee

Courtly charges a platform fee of ₱10 per successfully paid and confirmed court-rental hour. The venue owner is responsible for this fee. The player pays the published court rental amount only; the platform fee is accounted for against the venue owner's earnings and included in the weekly Courtly invoice.

3. Paid Open Play transaction fee

A ₱10 Transaction Fee is added to each paid Open Play registration checkout. The player bears this fee. Where the venue receives the player's full payment directly through its own GCash, Maya, QR Ph or other merchant account, the venue owner receives and holds the Transaction Fee on Courtly's behalf and must remit it to Courtly. The Transaction Fee is not venue registration revenue.

Free Open Play registrations do not incur a Transaction Fee. No fee is charged for a free registration.

4. Venue payment accounts and verification

The venue owner is responsible for providing payment instructions and a valid payment QR or merchant details that the venue is authorized to use. Unless Courtly has separately enabled an approved integrated payment provider for the venue, payments made through the venue's QR are received directly by the venue. Courtly does not represent that it has automatically verified such payments. The venue owner must check their merchant account before confirming a booking or registration.

5. Weekly fee statements and invoices

Courtly generates itemized invoices every Friday. Invoices may include court-rental platform fees, player-paid Open Play Transaction Fees collected on Courtly's behalf, applicable credits or adjustments, and prior outstanding balances. The venue owner agrees to remit the total amount shown on the invoice.

6. Payment deadline

Each weekly invoice is payable within seventy-two (72) hours from its actual issue timestamp. The invoice in the Owner Portal shows the issue date, exact due date, amount due and itemized transactions. Courtly may send payment reminders using the contact details on the venue account.

7. Remittance submission and verification

The venue owner must submit the remittance amount, payment method and transaction reference through the Owner Portal. Supporting proof may be requested. A submission is marked pending until Courtly verifies that funds have arrived in Courtly's designated receiving account. A submitted reference alone is not proof that funds cleared. If a remittance is rejected or is insufficient to cover the outstanding balance, the unpaid amount remains due.

8. Non-payment and temporary listing suspension

If an invoice remains unpaid after the 72-hour deadline and there is no sufficient remittance awaiting verification, Courtly may temporarily unpublish or suspend the venue listing. During suspension, the venue will not appear in public venue discovery, and new court bookings and Open Play registrations will be disabled.

Suspension does not delete the venue, the owner's account, financial records or existing reservations. The owner may continue to access the Owner Portal, view invoices, submit remittances and manage information. Existing confirmed customer bookings must be honored unless separately resolved under the applicable cancellation/refund policy.

9. Reactivation

After Courtly verifies payment of all overdue invoices, the listing may be reactivated. Courtly restores the venue's previous public-listing state; if the venue was unpublished by its owner before suspension, payment alone will not republish it.

10. Invoice disputes, credits and refunds

Venue owners should raise invoice disputes promptly and provide the relevant booking or registration references and supporting information. Verified duplicates, cancellations, refunds and billing errors may be corrected through a credit or adjustment. Undisputed amounts remain payable by the invoice deadline.

11. Venue owner responsibilities

  • Keep venue information, court availability, prices, operating hours and payment instructions accurate.
  • Use only payment accounts and QR codes the venue is authorized to use.
  • Verify payments in the actual merchant account before confirming bookings and Open Play registrations.
  • Honor confirmed bookings and communicate cancellations or changes to players.
  • Remit Courtly fees in full and on time.
  • Keep owner login credentials secure and notify Courtly of unauthorized access.

12. Acceptance and changes

The owner accepts the terms by selecting “I agree and accept” in the Courtly Owner Portal. Courtly records the accepted terms version, venue and timestamp. If Courtly publishes a materially updated fee policy or terms version, the owner may be asked to accept the new version before new transactions are accepted.
