Using Courtly

Booking terms

Updated 7 November 2026

Courtly helps players discover venues and coaches, request court bookings and join Open Play. Read the listing's own payment and cancellation instructions before you book.

1. Your account

Provide accurate account and contact information, use an email you can access, and keep your sign-in details private. Use your own player account for bookings and registrations. Do not impersonate another person, submit false payment proof, misuse someone else's information or interfere with other users' bookings.

2. Reservations and temporary holds

Review the court or event, local date and time, duration and total before continuing. Payment instructions are shown after a temporary hold is created for eligible court and paid Open Play selections. The checkout shows when that hold expires.

A hold is not a confirmed reservation. Upload your payment proof while the hold is active. If time runs out, check your dashboard and contact the venue before sending money. Availability may change after an unused hold expires. Free Open Play registration does not require a payment proof or Convenience Fee.

3. Payments and confirmation

Payments are made using the venue's or coach's instructions and receiving account. The venue or coach manually reviews submitted payment proof. “Awaiting verification” means that review is still pending; “Confirmed” means the operator has approved the booking or registration in Courtly.

Check the recipient and amount in your payment app before approving a transfer. Keep the payment reference and your Courtly booking reference. A payment record or email from Courtly reflects the operator's recorded approval; it does not independently verify the bank or wallet transfer and is not a tax invoice. Ask the receiving business for any official invoice you need.

5. Cancellations, rescheduling and refunds

Contact the venue or coach directly about a change or cancellation. Their stated rules and your rights under applicable law govern any refund or rescheduling request. Ask about those rules before paying if the listing does not explain them.

Changing a booking to “Cancelled” or rejecting a payment proof in Courtly does not automatically send a refund. The receiving venue or coach must check the transfer and arrange any agreed refund. Do not submit a second payment to resolve a delayed approval without checking with the recipient first.

6. Listings and activities

Venue owners, coaches and organisers provide their listing details, schedules and payment instructions. Confirm equipment, access, facilities and activity requirements with them when these affect your visit. Follow the venue's rules and treat other players and staff respectfully.

7. Help and your information

Use Help & account support to report a technical issue or send Courtly a private account request. For urgent booking problems, use the venue or coach's contact details. The Privacy Notice explains the information used by the service and available account controls.
